V | Academy 3.11.0 - Sidebar Navigation & Profile Dock

This is a PLATFORM PATCH. Do not delete or replace the whole repository.

Replace these files in your existing v-academy repository:

  js/app.js
  js/config.js
  css/academy.css
  package.json
  CHANGELOG.md
  README(1).md

Then commit and push to main. Cloudflare should deploy automatically.

After deployment, Ctrl + F5 and confirm:

1. Sidebar primary navigation is now:
   - Home
   - Bulletin Board
   - Learn
   - Tools & Workflows

2. Progress and Settings are no longer primary menu items.

3. Profile is anchored at the bottom of the sidebar.
   - Click name/role to open Profile & Settings.
   - Click the progress bar/text to open Progress.

4. Pinned tools appear between primary navigation and the profile dock.
   - Up to 5 pins are shown.
   - If more than 5 are pinned, View all appears.
   - Opening or unpinning a visible item still works.

5. Version text shows 3.11.0.

No Apps Script, Google Sheet, content JSON or reporting changes are required for this patch.
